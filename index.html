Our Story

A family promise,
poured into every bottle.

Pradeebha Oil Trading Company began in 1989 with a simple belief — the oil in your kitchen should be as honest as the food you cook with it. Decades later, that belief lives on as Nam Arokiyam, which means “Our Health”.

We still press the old way: carefully chosen seeds, crushed slowly in a wooden marachekku so the oil never overheats. What reaches your table keeps its natural aroma, colour and goodness — exactly as nature made it.

The Chekku Way

From seed to seal,
nothing rushed.

Refined oils are made with heat and solvents. Ours are made with time.

  1. 01

    Select

    Only clean, mature seeds and copra make the cut — sorted by hand.

  2. 02

    Sun-dry

    Dried naturally under the sun to bring out the true flavour of the seed.

  3. 03

    Wood-press

    Crushed slowly in a wooden chekku. Low friction, low heat — nutrients stay in.

  4. 04

    Settle

    Left to rest and settle naturally. No bleaching, no refining, no shortcuts.

  5. 05

    Seal

    Filtered, bottled and sealed fresh in 500 ml and 1 litre packs.
