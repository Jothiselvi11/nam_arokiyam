how it started

We still do it
the slow way.

Pradeebha Oil Trading Company started in 1989, selling oil the plain old way: pressed in a wooden chekku, poured into bottles, nothing else done to it. We’ve grown since then, but we haven’t found a good reason to change that part. Today the oil goes out under the name Nam Arokiyam, which means “our health”.

The marachekku turns slowly, so the oil never gets hot enough to lose its smell or colour. Open a bottle of our gingelly oil and it smells of sesame. If you’ve only cooked with refined oil, you’ll notice the difference the first time you temper mustard seeds in it.

how it’s made

Not complicated.
Just slow.

Refineries use heat and solvents to squeeze out every last drop. We’re happy to leave a little behind.

  1. 01

    Select

    Only clean, mature seeds and copra make the cut — sorted by hand.

  2. 02

    Sun-dry

    Dried naturally under the sun to bring out the true flavour of the seed.

  3. 03

    Wood-press

    Crushed slowly in a wooden chekku. Low friction, low heat — nutrients stay in.

  4. 04

    Settle

    Left to rest and settle naturally. No bleaching, no refining, no shortcuts.

  5. 05

    Seal

    Filtered, bottled and sealed fresh in 500 ml and 1 litre packs.

What refining does

  • Extracted with high heat
  • Chemical solvents used
  • Bleached & deodorised
  • Natural aroma lost

What we do instead
